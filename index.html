About the case

Break something. Watch Telltale work out what.

The simulator rewrites the last few hours of data, state by state, the way the real failure would. Telltale is never told what you broke. It sees only hourly metrics by state, the festival calendar, the change log (which you can leave empty or fill with decoys) and what other advertisers are seeing. Pick “Mystery” to test yourself against it.

Telltale is a concept prototype for the Media.net APM case study (alerting on ad campaign anomalies). Every advertiser, person and number is simulated in your browser. Built by Subhranil Hazra.
